This is an artwork by Ed Fornieles. While you take part, a voice in an earpiece — sometimes an AI, sometimes the artist — will give you directions: where to go, what to look at, what to do or say. You choose whether to follow them.

**You can stop at any time.** Take the earpiece out, or tell the voice you want to stop, and hand the phone back at the desk. You don't need to give a reason.

## Safety

- Don't do anything that feels unsafe or that you don't want to do, whatever the voice says.
- Stay inside the gallery and away from [stairs / the artworks marked as off limits / …].
- If you feel unwell or need help, stop and speak to any member of staff.

## What is recorded

While you take part we record:

- **Sound:** what you say into the earpiece microphone, and what is said to you.
- **Video:** cameras in the gallery film the space and the people in it.
- **The room:** microphones in the gallery pick up sound in the space.
- **This registration:** your name, email address, and the photo you take now.

## How it is used

- To make the artwork, and films, images, sound and texts that come out of it. These may be exhibited, published online, used in press and kept in the artist's archive.
- Recordings are edited by the artist's studio, with help from AI software.
- **AI services:** what you say is turned into text, and the AI's replies are written, by OpenAI (a company based in the USA), so your voice recordings and the conversation are sent to OpenAI while you take part. [Check OpenAI's data-retention terms for your account and state them here.]
- Your email is used only to contact you about this project. It is not published or shared.

Recordings and your details are kept by [Ed Fornieles Studio, address] for [how long / until you ask us to delete them].

## Your choices

You can ask to see what we hold about you, or to have your recordings removed from future use, by emailing [contact email]. Work that has already been exhibited or published may not be possible to withdraw.

## Face matching (optional)

If you tick the separate box, we use face-matching software on the gallery footage, comparing it with your photo, so we can find which footage you appear in. This uses your face as biometric data. It runs on the studio's own computer and is used only to sort the footage of this project. If you don't tick it, you can still take part; we'll match footage by hand.
