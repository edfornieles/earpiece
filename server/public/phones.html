Connect a phone

Loading connection link…
  1. Open the link on this phone.
    Scan the QR code or enter the link in its browser.
  2. Tap Enable audio.
    Allow microphone access. Keep the phone powered with its screen on.
  3. Pair its earpiece.
    Check that both playback and microphone use that earpiece.
  4. Test at the control desk.
    Start a test visit, talk to this phone, and check its microphone return. End the test visit before handing over.

Waiting for a phone to connect.

After setup

Assign a registered participant to this phone in the control room. The desk starts and ends their visit; their microphone is muted between visits.

Participant audio returns only to the desk and recording. It is never relayed to another participant.
