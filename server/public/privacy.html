Mother — privacy

Mother is a private performance system run by Ed Fornieles. Only people Ed has invited can sign in to direct it.

Signing in with Google

When you sign in with Google, Mother receives your email address and name from your Google account. It uses them only to check that you are on the list of invited directors and to show your name to other directors while you are connected. Mother does not receive your password, contacts, files or anything else from your Google account.

What is kept

  • Your email, name and role (director or owner) on the list of invited people, until Ed removes you.
  • When you last connected, and a log of what you did on the director's desk during a recorded session, as part of that session's record.

None of this is sold or shared with anyone else.

Removing your access

Ask Ed to remove you, and your access and stored details on the list are deleted. You can also remove Mother's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
