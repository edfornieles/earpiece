Cue guide

Speech plays a passage. Silence begins after the previous cue finishes playing. Wait for desk holds until you press Continue cue. Question waits for one reply. Conversation allows a limited exchange. Wait for role waits for a specific cue or the end of another role’s scene.

Exact pauses use Silence cues. Hesitant delivery can be described on adaptable passages; preview the wording before the performance. Readiness cues require a reply or your confirmation—Mother does not infer physical movement automatically.
